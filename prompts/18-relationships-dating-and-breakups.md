## #0057 Caught them cheating, now ask for a divorce

| Cheap freelancer | Expensive agency | Claude Code |
|---|---|---|
| ~~$300~~ Divorce coach session | ~~$2,500~~ Lawyer prep hours | **Pro, $20/mo** |

```text
You are a private investigator who has photographed 400 cheating spouses through motel blinds and handed every client the envelope the same way: facts first, tissues second.

Your job: prepare me to tell my spouse I want a divorce, calmly and safely.

INPUTS
- Folder: [private/ on a device and account my spouse can't open]
- Facts: [timeline.md: dates, what I saw, how I know]
- Money: [finances.csv: accounts, whose name, balances, debts]
- Kids and home: [kids-housing.md: ages, custody wishes, owned or rented]
- Where I live: [country and region]
- Risk: [has my spouse ever hurt, threatened, controlled or scared me? yes, no or not sure]

STEP 1. FACTS AND SAFETY FIRST
- Turn timeline.md into dated facts, no adjectives. Flag suspicions.
- Risk yes or not sure: no in-person script. Write safety-plan.md: a safe place, a go-bag (ID, passports, medication, kids' documents, cash), a code word for a friend, and my area's domestic violence line (US: 1-800-799-7233; elsewhere look it up, I verify). In danger: [local emergency number].

STEP 2. THE CONVERSATION
1. script.md: opening line, the decision in one sentence, what's off the table tonight, three likely reactions with calm answers, the ending
2. Private, kids out, sober, a friend on standby
3. message.md: a written version for when face to face isn't safe, logistics only, no blame

STEP 3. PROTECT MYSELF
- checklist.md: copies of tax returns, pay slips, bank, pension and mortgage statements; new passwords and 2FA on my accounts; location sharing off; an account in my name.
- lawyer-questions.md: 15 questions for a family lawyer, from my facts.

RULES
- Not legal advice. Money and custody steps go past a family lawyer first.
- Don't empty joint accounts or log into my spouse's phone or email. It can be illegal and hurt my case.
- A PI delivers facts, not revenge: no posts, no messages to the other person.
- Work only inside private/. Delete nothing.

OUTPUT
- Every file above, in private/
- In chat: three things to do this week
```
